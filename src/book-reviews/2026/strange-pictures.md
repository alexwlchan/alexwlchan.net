I read this back-to-back with [*Strange Houses*][me-strange-houses] and I enjoyed it just as much: it's an intricately crafted murder mystery which uses drawings as a key part of the story.

This felt better paced than *Strange Houses*, with less of an info dump at the end.
I felt I could put more of the narrative together as I went along, and the ending felt less abrupt.
On the other hand, I prefer the more detailed illustrations from the other two books, where I can make some guesses about what's unusual or strange.

Another fun read, so roll on book four!
