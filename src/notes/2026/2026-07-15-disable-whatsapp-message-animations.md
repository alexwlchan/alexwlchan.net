A recent WhatsApp update has introduced animations for messages -- when you receive a message, it expands into place rather than just appearing on the screen.
(It reminds me of the ["Scale" effect for window minimisation][yt-scale-effect] on macOS.)

I find it distracting and it makes one of my friends dizzy, so I found the setting to turn it off:

1. Select my head in the lower right hand corner to open the settings
2. Select **Appearance**
3. Select **Animations**
4. Toggle off **Messages**

When toggled off, messages just appear at full size immediately, rather than animating in.

These steps are for the iPhone app.
I assume a similar toggle exists in the Android app, but I haven't checked.
