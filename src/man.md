I publish a small number of macOS man pages under `/man/`, so I have some nicely-formatted pages I can refer to in my posts.
I only publish the subset of pages I refer to; not a complete copy of Apple's documentation.

This is inspired by Dr. Drang, who [did the same thing][leancrew-html-man-pages], but I wrote my own scripts.

Everything under `/man/` is excluded by [my `robots.txt` file][me-robots-txt], so these pages won't show up in search engines.
